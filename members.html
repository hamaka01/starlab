강민규Mingyu Kang Mingyu Kang, Ph.D.강민규 · Ph.D.

서울시립대학교 도시행정학과 교수이자 도시과학빅데이터·AI연구원(UBAI) 빅데이터연구센터장입니다. University of Washington에서 Urban Design and Planning 박사학위를 받았고, 국토연구원을 거쳐 2020년부터 서울시립대학교에 재직하고 있습니다. 국토도시정책, 도시데이터사이언스, 데이터과학기반행정, 토지주택부동산을 연구합니다.

Professor of Urban Administration at the University of Seoul and Director of the Big Data Research Center at UBAI. He received his Ph.D. in Urban Design and Planning from the University of Washington and worked at the Korea Research Institute for Human Settlements before joining the University of Seoul in 2020. His research covers territorial and urban policy, urban data science, data-driven public administration, and land, housing and real estate.
